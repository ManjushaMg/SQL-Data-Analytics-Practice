E-Commerce customer churn analysis Project

This project delve into customer churn within an e-commerce domain, and by analysing customer attributes such as tenure,preferred payment modes,satisfaction scores, and purchase behaviour the project seeks to understand the customer loss and their turnover. 


Dataset used:https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1iKKCze_Fpk2n_g3BIZBiSjcDFdFcEn3D

Data contains: customer id,City Tier, Preferred payment mode,gender,satisfaction score etc.

Steps followed
1.Data cleaning(missing values,outliers,inconsistencies)
2.Data Transformation(column renaming,creating new column,)
3.Data Exploration and Analysis(finding out customer attributes such as average satisfaction score,preferred order category etc.)

Key Insights
1.customer who have churned=948 and active customer count is4680
2.average tenure is 3.17yrs
3.city tier with the highest number of churned customers whose preferred order category is Laptop & Accessory is  tier 3
4.most preferred payment mode in active  customer is debitcard
5,Tier 1 is the city with most number of customers
6.average satisfaction score of customer who have complained is2.9
